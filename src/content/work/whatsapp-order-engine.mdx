  A restaurant was taking orders over WhatsApp and typing each one into a sheet
  by hand. We rebuilt the entire order journey as three connected n8n
  workflows. Intake captures the message and creates a unique Order ID. An AI

  items and incomplete orders before they reach the kitchen — then logs
  everything to Google Sheets and alerts the manager. The fulfilment workflow
  sends the customer a confirmation, tracks delivery status, and fires an
  automated review request once the order is complete. Nothing is entered
  twice, nothing slips through.
